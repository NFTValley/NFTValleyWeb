How it works
From raw trait images to a full collection with metadata — in 4 steps
1
Name your project
Create a project, set collection name, size up to 100,000, canvas size and output format (PNG or JPG).
2
Upload trait layers
Add layers (Background, Body, Eyes, Hat…). Upload PNG/JPG trait images per layer and assign rarity weights.
3
Generate & preview
Choose Exact, Random, or Random + Fixed generation. Shuffle preview, regenerate unlimited rounds until satisfied.
4
Pay & export
Pay with USDT (TRC-20 or ERC-20). Download all images + metadata.json + OpenSea CSV + Scores + Rarity files.
